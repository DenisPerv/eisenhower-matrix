TasksBoard privacy

HomeOS TasksBoard, a small task board run privately by Maksym Denysiuk. Last updated 2026-10-30.

What we store

We store your email, name and the items you create. When you sign in with Google we receive only your email address, your name and your profile picture (the openid, email and profile scopes). We do not read your Gmail, contacts, calendar or files.

If you ask for access on the request page, we also use your email address to send you one email with the decision (approved or not), from Maksym's Gmail (maximdenisuk034@gmail.com).

Who sees it

Only people you share a list with see them. A new account starts with its own private list. Nobody else sees your lists, items or tags until you share a list or a tag list with them and they accept.

Where it lives

Your data is stored in a Supabase database and the page is served by Cloudflare Pages. Sign-in uses Google and Supabase. Nothing is sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your account

Ask Maksym to delete your account: maximdenisuk034@gmail.com. Your account, your lists and your items are then removed.
